Nurturing Every Step With Love, Wonder & Safety

Welcome to Elingas Buzzy Hub (www.elingasbuzzyhub.in). This dedicated childcare and activity center in Madipakkam provides a joyful, secure atmosphere where children play, rest, and celebrate early childhood.

At Elingas Buzzy Hub, we believe childhood should be filled with wonder, play, and emotional reassurance. Whether your child joins us for an energetic hour in our Play Area, a private game session with family, or a full day of attentive care in our Day Care nursery, their happiness and safety are our primary mission.

  • ✓ 100% Child-Safe Environment with CCTV & Sanitized Play Zones
  • ✓ 1:5 Caregiver Ratio with Warm, Experienced Mentors
  • ✓ Transparent Parent Updates with our Signature Personal Diary
  • ✓ Centrally Located in Madipakkam, Chennai for Busy Families
